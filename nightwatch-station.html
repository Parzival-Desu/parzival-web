NIGHTWATCH STATION - INSTRUCTIONS

OBJECTIVE

Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM without being caught by the animatronics.

CONTROLS

  • SPACE or Monitor Button - Toggle security camera system
  • A or ◄ Door - Close/open left door
  • D or Door ► - Close/open right door
  • Q or ◄ Light - Check left hallway (momentary)
  • E or Light ► - Check right hallway (momentary)
  • Camera Buttons - Switch between different room views

HOW ANIMATRONICS ATTACK

1. They move through rooms - Use cameras to track them. Rooms with animatronics show a red ! indicator.

2. They reach your doors - You'll hear footsteps and see "♪ SOUND" on the left or right side.

3. Visual warnings appear - Red "THREAT DETECTED" warnings show when they're at your door.

4. Check with lights - Press hallway lights to confirm if someone's there (you'll see/hear them).

5. CLOSE THE DOOR! - If an animatronic is at your door and it's open, they'll attack after ~5 seconds.

6. Wait it out - Keep the door closed until they leave, then open it to save power.

POWER MANAGEMENT

  • Everything uses power (shown as USAGE: 1-6)
  • Using cameras = +1 usage
  • Each closed door = +1 usage
  • Each light on = +1 usage (momentary)
  • If power hits 0%, you lose!

STRATEGY TIPS

  • Check cameras regularly but don't leave monitor up constantly
  • Use lights sparingly - only when you hear sounds
  • Close doors only when threats are present
  • Balance monitoring vs. power conservation
  • Animatronics get faster and more aggressive each hour
