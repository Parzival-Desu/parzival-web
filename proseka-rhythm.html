📖 How to Play

Objective: Hit the notes when they reach the bottom line!

  • D, F, J, K - Hit notes in each lane
  • ESC - Pause game
  • Perfect: Hit exactly on time (100 points)
  • Good: Hit close to time (50 points)
  • Bad: Hit late (10 points)
  • Miss: Didn't hit the note (0 points)

Keep your combo high to maximize your score! 🔥
